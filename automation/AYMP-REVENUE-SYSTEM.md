# AYMP Revenue System v1

## Principle
Value -> Trust -> Useful Paid Service -> Sustainable Revenue

## User journey
1. Discover a free AYMP Formula Insight.
2. Read related useful content.
3. Explore the AYMP Method.
4. Choose a service when a real need exists.
5. Submit an enquiry or booking request.
6. Receive the appropriate guidance or consultation.

## Revenue layers
- Free Formula Insights: audience and trust.
- Basic Guidance: focused paid guidance.
- Detailed Personal Analysis: structured personalized analysis.
- Premium Consultation: direct expert consultation.

## Measurement
Revenue performance is measured from:
Visitors x Engagement Rate x Lead Rate x Conversion Rate x Average Customer Value.

No revenue or token-value outcome is guaranteed.

## Token position
AYMP and 999xyz999 may be introduced only as optional future ecosystem utilities. They must not be presented as guaranteed-return or guaranteed-price products.

## Safety
The existing security layer and human approval principle remain in force. This document defines the business model only; it does not automatically collect payments, publish financial claims, or change token contracts.
